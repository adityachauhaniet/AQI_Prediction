About Us

What is AQI?

Air Quality Index (AQI) is a measurement system used to communicate how polluted the air currently is or how polluted it is forecast to become. It focuses on health effects people may experience within a few hours or days after breathing polluted air.

Why Air Quality Matters

Poor air quality can affect everyone — children, elderly people, and those with respiratory or heart conditions are especially vulnerable. Long-term exposure to polluted air increases the risk of serious health issues.

Who We are?

We are a dedicated team of environmental enthusiasts committed to providing accurate and real-time air quality data to the public.

AQI is created with a simple but powerful idea – to let everyone see what they are breathing. Knowing what's in the air is the first step towards enjoying clear air and a healthier life.

We are an open-source air quality monitoring platform that shows real-time air quality data of your location. We just not only provide numbers to check, but we also provide plans to save yourself from air pollution.

AQI is a platform that helps you stay informed by keeping an eye on air pollution levels in your city or monitoring the air in your home or workplace. Thus, we also offer a dashboard for users of Prana Air monitors. It allows them to easily track and analyze air quality data of their monitors.

What This Platform Does

  • Provides air quality awareness
  • Helps users understand pollution sources
  • Predicts AQI using machine learning
  • Shares global air quality reports

Our Mission

To provide accurate and real-time air quality data to help individuals make informed decisions about their health and environment.
